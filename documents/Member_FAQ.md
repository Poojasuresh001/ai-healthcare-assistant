# Member and Operations FAQ

> **Synthetic training material.** Northstar Benefit Services and every identifier, date, policy, and answer in this document are fictional. This is not real member guidance or a coverage determination.

## How does an existing group renewal work in this example?

The case owner opens a renewal case, collects the signed renewal election or amendment and the required roster and plan-selection materials, checks dates and identifiers, records the approved selection, and requests a second-person configuration review. See the Group Renewal Guide for the sample timeline and detailed checks.

## What documents are listed for a renewal?

The sample checklist calls for a signed renewal election or contract amendment, a current enrollment roster or signed no-change attestation, an employer contribution schedule, a plan-selection worksheet, and written eligibility-class changes when applicable. The case owner records missing or inconsistent items as open work.

## What happens if the requested renewal date does not match the signed document?

Set the case to `NEEDS_RECONCILIATION`, record the conflicting values, and pause configuration and member changes. The assigned Group Account Manager must provide a written resolution. The assistant must not choose the date.

## What is a plan effective date?

In this fictional training system, it is the start date stored for a plan version. For a particular group, read the date from the structured plan record and compare it with the approved source document. Do not infer the date from a plan name or from this FAQ.

## Can the assistant tell me whether a real claim is covered?

No. This project is a learning demonstration using fictional records. It may summarize an existing synthetic claim status returned from the database, but it does not make coverage decisions, interpret real plan contracts, or provide medical advice.

## What if no group, member, or claim is found?

The assistant should state that it found no matching synthetic record and ask the user to check the fictional identifier. It must not manufacture a record or substitute a similar identifier.

## May I test the assistant with real member data?

No. Use only the fabricated identifiers and rows supplied with this project. Do not upload company documents, production extracts, or real member, claim, or provider information.
